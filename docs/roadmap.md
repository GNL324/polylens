# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Current Capabilities

- Polymarket wallet analytics
- Polymarket/Kalshi market matching
- Sportsbook odds and futures ingestion
- Player prop arbitrage scanning
- Continuous monitoring
- SQLite persistence
- Telegram alerting

## Phase 2: Multi-Book Arbitrage

- Broader sport coverage
- More robust stale odds detection
- Book-specific availability diagnostics
- More precise stake sizing by odds format and payout rules

## Phase 3: Cross-Market Arbitrage

- Stronger settlement-rule modeling
- Field/NO leg discovery across venues
- Cross-exchange arbitrage confidence scoring
- Market-specific fee and slippage modeling

## Phase 4: AI-Powered Market Intelligence

- Market clustering and semantic matching
- News/event-aware market discovery
- Wallet clustering and behavior models
- Watchlist generation and opportunity explanations

## Phase 5: Execution Automation

- Execution simulation and paper trading
- Risk limits and bankroll management
- Broker/exchange integrations where legally and technically appropriate
- Human-in-the-loop approvals before any real execution
